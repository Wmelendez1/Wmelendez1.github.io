ABOUT ME

Data, systems, and better decisions.

I'm Walter Melendez, an Admissions Operations Analyst and Computer Science graduate who builds analytics, reporting, and automation solutions that make complex operational data easier to trust and act on.

What I Do

My work sits at the intersection of data analytics, business intelligence, and operations. I use SQL Server to investigate and reconcile data, Tableau to communicate performance, and Python, Excel, VBA, and Power Query to automate repeatable workflows.

I regularly work with Salesforce and contact-center data, lead funnels, transfer activity, agent performance, routing behavior, scheduling, and data-quality rules. I enjoy taking an ambiguous business question and turning it into a reproducible analysis or tool.

Education & Direction

I earned a B.S. in Computer Science from Full Sail University in 2024 and completed a Data Science Master Certificate in 2026. My graduate capstone focused on predictive lead-conversion modeling and strengthened my experience with machine learning, model evaluation, and communicating analytical results.

I'm continuing to grow toward data analyst, business intelligence, operations analytics, and data science opportunities where I can combine technical problem-solving with measurable business impact.
